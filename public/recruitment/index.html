How We Review Applications

Every governor has a story

We take the time to get to know each applicant.

Our leadership team reviews historical KvK performance, account development, activity and contributions to previous kingdoms using established analytical tools, including HeroScrolls.

This helps us better understand each governor's experience, consistency and potential contribution to Kingdom 3903.
