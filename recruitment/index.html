03

How We Review Applications

Every governor has a story

We believe a governor's value goes beyond power and kill points.

Our leadership team reviews each application individually, considering historical KvK performance, account development, activity and contributions to previous kingdoms.

Using established analytical tools, including HeroScrolls, we aim to better understand each applicant's experience, consistency and potential contribution to Kingdom 3903.

Our goal is to welcome reliable teammates who are ready to fight, grow and succeed together.
04

KvK & Migration Requirements

We fight together. We grow together.

Kingdom 3903 has clear expectations regarding KvK participation, teamwork and individual contributions.

We have established performance requirements to support a fair and balanced environment for our entire community.

Every application is reviewed individually. Specific requirements and expectations are discussed directly with applicants during the recruitment process.

We value dedication, communication, reliability and a willingness to contribute to the kingdom's success.
